How is σ calculated?
  1. A neural network outlines each vertebra on the X-ray.
  2. The centre of each vertebra becomes a point, and a smooth curve is fitted through them.
  3. Compare the curve's true length s with the straight line between its ends d.

σ = (1 − d / s)² — a straight spine gives 0, and σ grows as the curve bends.

Arc length and chord are measured in pixels of the 512×512 resized image (PNG/JPEG carries no pixel spacing). Est. Cobb's angle is the change in tangent angle along the middle 80% of the fitted curve — approximate and unvalidated. Severity bands were matched to a mask-derived Cobb proxy on a scoliosis-only dataset.
